# 90-second demo and judge questions

00–12s: Open 3D Holy Fire terrain and introduce evidence-linked camera network.
12–25s: Ask “Where was smoke first visible, and can another camera corroborate it?”
25–40s: Agent searches, selects camera A, shows a real clip at cited time.
40–58s: Agent explicitly decides to check a second camera; map flies there, opens clip B.
58–73s: Display aligned observations and, only if calibrated, bearing wedges with uncertainty.
73–85s: Open findings timeline, observed vs inferred vs unresolved, click evidence citation.
85–90s: “An agent that navigates video evidence in geographic context, not a chatbot attached to a map.”

## Honest response to judges
- **Why video agents?** Autonomous selection and inspection of temporally situated visual evidence; without the agent the user manually scrubs videos and compares views.
- **Is this triangulation reliable?** Only when locations, camera orientations, synchronized views and plume target are verified; otherwise show constraints, not point claims.
- **Why VAST?** Custom video storage, semantic retrieval and provenance of indexed segments.
- **Why Cosmos?** Temporal visual interpretation and event-level evidence extraction.
- **Why Weave?** Inspectable reasoning/tool traces and latency/failure evaluation.
- **Is this emergency-ready?** No, research prototype and historical incident exploration only.
